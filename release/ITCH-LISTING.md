## Full description

**Miss perfectly.**

The target is a trap.

In ONE OFF, the right answer is always exactly **one away**. Target 73? Land on 72 or 74. Hit 73 and you lose the round.

Play five tiny challenges across four ways to get it wrong:

- **Pick One:** choose the perfect miss.
- **Type It:** find your own number.
- **Slide It:** stop one away.
- **Quick Tap:** start the countdown, then catch the right moment. One pass only.

Chase a **5/5 PERFECTLY WRONG** run, keep a daily streak, and beat your fastest perfect time. There is a fresh Daily challenge each local calendar day, plus unlimited Practice runs whenever you want another try.

Share your score without spoiling the numbers. Daily replays keep your original completed score.

**Play in your browser. No account required inside the game.** Your records stay in that browser; they do not sync between devices or between itch.io and the web version.

Created by **lcsmith2009**, with ChatGPT assistance in code and text. The game runs locally using ordinary number-generation code; it does not call a generative AI service during play.

## How to play / controls

Land exactly one above or below the target. Every run has five rounds; a wrong answer loses that round and you continue.

- Tap/click a choice, or use Tab and Enter/Space.
- Type a whole number and choose LOCK IT or press Enter.
- Drag the slider. Keyboard: arrow keys adjust it; Home/End move to its limits. Choose LOCK IT.
- Quick Tap: choose TAP TO START. After GET READY, watch the changing number and activate the button when it is exactly one away. The sequence runs once.
- Choose NEXT after each answer and SEE SCORE after round five.
- Use SHARE RESULT, PRACTICE AGAIN, or HOME from the result screen.

Run time includes reading, waiting, and feedback. A speed record requires a perfect 5/5. Daily challenges use your device's local date; the first completed score for that date stays fixed. Clearing site data can erase your records. Storage restrictions may prevent saving; play and sharing still work.

Quick Tap is a visual timing challenge. Desktop keyboard and narrow browser layouts were tested; physical Android/TalkBack, iOS/Safari, and native phone sharing have not been certified.
