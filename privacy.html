Simple by design.

ONE OFF currently does not require an account and does not send your game history to our own server. Scores, streaks and personal records are stored locally in your browser. Clearing browser/site data may erase them.

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages, so the hosting provider may process standard technical information needed to deliver the website.

If analytics, payments, accounts or other data collection are added later, this notice will be updated before those features launch.
