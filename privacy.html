PRIVACY

Simple by design.

ONE OFF does not require an account or send your game history to our own server. Scores, streaks and personal records stay in your browser. Clearing browser/site data may erase them. Records are separate for each browser, device and host, including GitHub Pages and itch.io.

The web version is hosted by GitHub Pages. If you play on itch.io, that platform hosts the game. Hosting providers may process technical information needed to deliver their services. Optional sharing sends your result only through the share action you choose.

The game includes no analytics, advertising or payment code. Any optional itch.io support payment is handled by that platform and its payment providers, under their own policies.
